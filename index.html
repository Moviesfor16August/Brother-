A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Milan,

Sabse pehle — HAPPY BIRTHDAY, BHAI! 🥳❤️

Aaj tera birthday hai, but honestly, mere liye ye sirf tera birthday nahi hai.

Ye woh din hai jab life ne mujhe ek aisa dost diya, jo time ke saath sirf “friend” nahi raha, balki family jaisa ban gaya.

Pata nahi humari dosti exactly kab itni strong ho gayi...

But ek cheez sure hai — tere saath bitaya hua har moment mere liye special hai.

Chahe hum kitna bhi bakchodi karein 😂, ek dusre ki bezzati karein, ya kabhi chhoti-moti arguments ho jaayein...

End mein apni dosti hamesha wahi rehti hai.

Milan, life mein bahut log milte hain.

Kuch log thodi der ke liye aate hain, kuch memories dekar chale jaate hain...

But kuch log aise hote hain jinke bina life imagine karna hi ajeeb lagta hai.

Tu ab mere liye mere family member jaisa hai. ❤️

Tu unhi logon mein se ek hai.

Main bas itna chahta hoon ki tu life mein jo bhi dream dekhe, woh pura ho.

Tu hamesha khush rahe, confident rahe aur kisi bhi situation mein apna asli version kabhi mat khona.

Aur haan...

Future mein chahe hum kitne bhi busy ho jaayein, kitni bhi responsibilities aa jaayein...

Bas ek cheez change nahi honi chahiye — apni dosti. 🫂

Thank you, bhai, meri life ka ek important part banne ke liye.

Thank you un countless memories ke liye jo humne saath banayi hain...

Aur un countless memories ke liye bhi jo abhi banani baaki hain.

Aaj tera day hai, toh enjoy kar full power! 🔥🎂

Once again,

Happy Birthday, Milan! ❤️🎉

May this year bring you success, happiness, crazy adventures, unforgettable memories and everything you truly deserve.

Stay the same crazy, annoying, amazing dost you are. 😂❤️
